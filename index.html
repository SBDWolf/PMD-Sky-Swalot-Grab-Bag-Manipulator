Swalot's Grab Bag Manipulator

A web tool to generate Quicksave manipulations for Swalot's Grab Bag

Read this article if you need more information on how these work

Happen to carry gummies? Set a quantity under Gummies to feed and the solver piggybacks gummi eats onto the same quicksave run: between buying items it eats gummies whose outcome matches your target stat (or omniboost). Eating consumes the gummi's rolls first, then advances the PRNG like any turn (+6/+7/+8/+9 by team size).

Fastest manipulation

Works in any dungeon from the same quicksave — no dungeon or team-size setup needed. Every action costs 1 turn. Let's go together (the partner follows you): swapping places advances the PRNG by 3, walking away by 4, standing still or walking next to them by 5 — and eating a gummi counts as a stand. Wait there (the partner stays put): swapping is still 3, and every other turn pass — walking around, standing, eating — is 4. Either way the gummi's rolls happen first (25% for a boost at all, then a 1-in-16 omniboost, otherwise one of the four stats) and the eat turn's own advance is applied after them. Each row lists the moves to perform before eating that gummi — the order of moves within a row doesn't matter. Already performed some turns? Enter the PRNG advances they cost under Advances before start.
